General Information

V1.0.0

1. About OpenLaut

OpenLaut is a browser-based chat interface provided by Laut Media ("we", "us"). It allows you to connect your own OpenRouter account and interact with artificial intelligence models of your choosing. OpenLaut is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All information you enter or generate in OpenLaut, including chats, settings, profiles, uploaded files, and your OpenRouter API key, is stored locally within your own web browser on your own device, using the browser's local storage facilities. This information is not stored on any server operated by us.

3. No Data Collection by Laut Media

Laut Media does not collect, receive, store, or have access to your chats, files, API key, or any other information you enter into OpenLaut. OpenLaut does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling tools, and does not install tracking cookies or similar technologies. This statement applies to the current version of OpenLaut.

4. Third-Party Services

To generate responses, OpenLaut sends your messages, attached content, and API key directly from your browser to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which may route your request to the AI model provider you select. We do not control these third parties. Their handling of your data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, which you should review before use. Usage charges are billed to your OpenRouter account by OpenRouter, and cost figures shown in OpenLaut are for reference only.

5. Recommended Browser

We recommend using Google Chrome (desktop) when using OpenLaut. Some browsers, including Safari, apply stricter limits to locally stored website data and may restrict or automatically clear it, which may cause saved chats and settings to be lost or the application to behave unexpectedly. We do not guarantee that OpenLaut will function correctly in other browsers.

6. Risk of Data Loss

Because your data is stored only in your browser, it may be permanently lost if you clear your browsing data, use private or incognito mode, change or reset your browser or device, or if your browser removes stored data automatically. We cannot recover lost data. You are responsible for exporting and backing up any information you wish to keep.

7. Security of Your API Key and Device

Your API key is stored in your browser and is not included in exported files. You are responsible for the security of your device and browser. Do not use OpenLaut on shared or public computers, and revoke your API key with OpenRouter if you suspect it has been exposed.

8. Estimate Input Token Counter

The Estimate Input Token Counter is an approximation only and does not include images. Actual token counts and charges are determined by the selected model and OpenRouter.

9. AI-Generated Content

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for your purpose. You are responsible for reviewing all outputs before relying on or publishing them, and for ensuring that content you submit does not infringe the rights of others or breach applicable laws, including laws on personal data.

10. Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted, Laut Media provides OpenLaut without warranties of any kind and is not liable for any loss or damage, including loss of data, arising from your use of OpenLaut or any third-party service.

11. Changes

We may update this information from time to time. Continued use of OpenLaut constitutes acceptance of the updated information.
